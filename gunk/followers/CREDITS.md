# Gen 5 follower sprites: credits

The overworld sprites for #494-649 in this folder are the community-made HGSS-style Unova overworlds, as packaged in
the Pokémon Essentials "Followers" sprite set. They are converted to the DS's size and 16 colours by
`tools/followers5.py`; the art itself is unchanged. They are fan art, used here non-commercially with credit.

The set's individual sprites don't carry their artists' names, so everyone credited for the set is listed here:

- **Unova overworlds (first released 2011 on DeviantArt):** hamsterSKULL (myumorph), Pokegirl4ever, Kyle-Dove,
  Phyromatical (the "All Gen 5 Overworld sprites" compilation).
- **Overworld sprites of the Essentials follower set:** WesleyFG, The Spriters Resource, 2and2makes5,
  Chocosrawlooid, cSc-A7X, Fernandojl, Gallanty, Getsuei-H, Gizamimi-Pichu, help-14, kdiamo11, Kid1513, Kymoyonian,
  Kyt666, LarryTurbo, Milomilotic11, MissingLukey, Pokegirl4ever, Silver-Skies, spritesstealer, Syledude,
  TyranitarDark, Zyon17 (credit list from Swdfm's "Overworld Swimming Sprites! 2" on Eevee Expo, which builds on
  the same set).

If you drew one of these sprites and want your name credited differently, or the sprite removed, please open an
issue.
